# Thoth product policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This policy covers how Thoth.app and the Chrome extension are meant to be used. Privacy details are in [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## What Thoth is

Thoth is a **local-first writing assistant**. It helps with grammar, rewrite, and generate on this computer and in Chrome. It does not require a Thoth account.

You own the text you enter. Suggestions are assistance only — review them before you send or publish.

## How processing works

- **Local mode (default):** writing stays on your machine (Thoth.app, `http://127.0.0.1:8000`, and Ollama).
- **API mode (optional):** if you paste your own key, rewrite/generate go through the local Thoth server to the provider you chose (for example OpenAI or Groq).

Thoth does not sell your writing or use your drafts to train public models.

## Your responsibilities

Use Thoth in line with:

- The sites and services you write in
- School, workplace, or client rules that apply to you
- The terms of any AI provider you configure in API mode

Do not use Thoth to break the law or to access accounts or data you are not allowed to use.

## No warranty

Thoth is provided as-is. Grammar and writing output can be wrong or incomplete. You are responsible for the text you submit.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the product changes. The in-app **Settings → Privacy & Policy** page and this file are the current copy.
